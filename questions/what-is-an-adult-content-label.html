What Is an Adult Content Label?

An adult content label is a descriptive category or tag used to communicate that material is intended for adults. Labels can vary between publishers and platforms and should not be treated as independent verification.

Context

This page is part of CFNM.si's terminology and web-literacy reference. It explains the language or online concept without hosting or reproducing adult material.
