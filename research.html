Research

Terminology Observatory

CFNM.si can develop into an independent reference project studying how niche acronyms and category labels are used on the public web.

Research areas

  • Acronym usage and variants
  • Search language and terminology changes
  • Domain naming patterns
  • Web literacy and privacy

Method

Research should use public, attributable sources and clearly distinguish observation from interpretation. We will not scrape copyrighted adult libraries or reproduce their material.

Future datasets

Planned additions include a terminology timeline, acronym relationship map and public-search trend dashboard when reliable source data is available.
