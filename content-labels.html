How Content Labels Work

Online categories often use short acronyms and labels to communicate a subject quickly. Those labels can be ambiguous, inconsistent or reused differently by different communities.

Label ≠ guarantee

A category name is a description, not independent verification of everything on a page. Users should consider the source, context and stated policies.

Why acronyms spread

Short acronyms are easy to type, remember and use as tags. Once an acronym becomes established, it can appear in search queries, domain names, navigation and metadata.

CFNM as a case study

CFNM is commonly used as an acronym for “clothed female, naked male.” CFNM.si documents that terminology as a reference subject without hosting the underlying adult material.
