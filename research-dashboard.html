What we can responsibly measure

  • Relative search-interest trends
  • Geographic concentration of interest
  • Related query language
  • Changes in terminology over time
  • Differences between search properties
  • Publicly documented industry and web-literacy signals

What we will not claim

  • Exact number of adult users from Trends scores
  • Gender or sexual identity of individual searchers
  • Private browsing behavior
  • Revenue, conversion or audience figures without a credible source
