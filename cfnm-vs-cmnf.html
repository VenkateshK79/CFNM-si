CFNM vs CMNF

CFNM and CMNF are contrasting acronyms describing which participant is clothed and which is nude.

CFNM

CFNM commonly expands to “clothed female, naked male.” The term is used as a niche adult/fetish classification and in related discussions.

CMNF

CMNF commonly expands to “clothed male, naked female.” It is the corresponding reverse arrangement of the two clothing descriptors.

Why the distinction matters

Because the acronyms are only four letters long, they are easy to confuse. Understanding the order of the letters makes the terminology clearer when reading a glossary, community discussion or content taxonomy.
