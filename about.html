About CFNM.si

CFNM.si is an independent reference project focused on terminology, online language, web literacy and research surrounding the CFNM acronym.

Purpose

The site exists to explain terminology and provide useful context without hosting or reproducing adult material.

What makes the project different

  • Searchable terminology rather than media aggregation.
  • Original reference pages rather than copied publisher descriptions.
  • Privacy and web-literacy material alongside terminology.
  • Research pages that identify sources and methodology.
  • Clear separation between editorial information and any future paid placement.

Editorial standard

We aim to correct factual errors, distinguish observations from interpretation and avoid sensational or unnecessary explicit detail.
