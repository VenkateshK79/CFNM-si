Editorial Policy

CFNM.si is an independent terminology and web-literacy project. Its purpose is to explain niche acronyms and related online concepts without hosting or reproducing adult material.

What we publish

  • Definitions and terminology explanations.
  • Original analysis and research based on public information.
  • Privacy, security and web-literacy guidance.
  • Transparent directory and sponsorship information.

What we do not publish

  • Explicit images, videos or downloadable adult material.
  • Scraped descriptions or copied articles from adult publishers.
  • Adult-site reviews presented as independent consumer endorsements.
  • Material involving minors, non-consensual sexual content or illegal sexual activity.

Independence

Listings or sponsorships, if introduced, will be identified. Paid placement does not mean editorial endorsement. We do not claim affiliation with businesses merely because their names or terminology appear in reference material.

Corrections

If a factual definition or source attribution is wrong, the site can be updated or corrected. Reference material should distinguish established facts from interpretation.
