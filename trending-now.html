Adult Web Trends Observatory

Trending Now

A live research view of recent Google Search surges by country. This is a discovery layer, not a measurement of individual users or exact market demand.

Source: Google Trends Trending Now. Google says this view covers 100+ countries and regions, refreshes on average every ten minutes, and provides bucketed search-volume labels and growth versus a predicted baseline.

Open Google Trending Now ↗

What this layer records

Recent surge
Queries experiencing unusual recent search interest.
Search-volume bucket
Google's displayed bucket such as 10K+, 100K+ or 1M+ — not an exact count.
Growth
Percentage growth versus Google's predicted baseline when provided.
Started
When the trend began and whether it remains active in the live interface.
Breakdown
Related queries that Google groups into a trend.
Country
The selected national/regional Trending Now view.

Research boundary

Trending Now is broad and news-driven. It should not be interpreted as a complete list of adult searches, nor should its traffic buckets be converted into exact search counts. The Observatory keeps source labels and provenance with collected observations.
