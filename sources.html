Sources & Methodology

CFNM.si aims to be a reference project, so definitions and research should be traceable rather than presented as unexplained authority.

How terminology is researched

  1. Identify the term or question being documented.
  2. Compare reliable public references where available.
  3. Prefer the most direct or authoritative source for factual claims.
  4. Record meaningful differences in wording rather than pretending every source uses identical definitions.
  5. Add original explanation and context instead of copying source text.

Research standards

Search-interest figures, when published, will be labeled as relative measurements and accompanied by their source, geography, period and methodology. We will not invent absolute traffic figures from normalized data.

Originality

CFNM.si does not copy adult-site descriptions, scrape galleries or reproduce copyrighted adult material. The project's value comes from organization, explanation, original research and web-literacy context.

Corrections

If a factual statement is shown to be wrong, the relevant page can be corrected and the change can be documented when material.
