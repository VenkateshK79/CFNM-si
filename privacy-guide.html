Privacy

Adult Website Privacy Guide

Adult-oriented websites can involve particularly sensitive browsing activity. This guide explains general privacy practices without recommending any particular service.

Before creating an account

  • Read the privacy policy and terms.
  • Check whether the service explains retention and deletion.
  • Use a unique password and enable multi-factor authentication where available.
  • Understand whether your profile is public, private or searchable.

Tracking checklist

Look for information about cookies, analytics, advertising identifiers, third-party services and account-linked activity. Browser privacy controls can reduce some tracking, but they do not make a user anonymous.

Payments

Before paying, verify the domain, connection security and billing description. Keep payment credentials away from unfamiliar forms.

Deletion and removal

A credible service should explain how users can close an account or request removal of information where applicable. Privacy rights vary by country.
